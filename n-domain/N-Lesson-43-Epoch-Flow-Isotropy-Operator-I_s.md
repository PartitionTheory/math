## 2. Defining the Epoch Flow Isotropy Operator

Let $V_s$ be the epoch flow vector field.

$$\mathcal{I}_s(E)=\mathcal{J}_s(E)-\mathcal{A}_s(E)$$

- $\mathcal{I}_s(E)<0$ → directional uniformity decreases

Isotropy captures **uniform directional behavior** of recursion flow.
