In N‑Lesson‑60 we introduced the potential conservation operator:

$$

Now we introduce the **epoch flow potential recursion potential**:

$$\mathcal{PP}_s$$

## 2. Defining the Epoch Flow Potential Recursion Potential

Let $\mathcal{P}_s$ be the recursion potential field.

The potential recursion potential is defined as:

$$
\mathcal{PP}_s(E)=-\mathcal{CP}_s(E)
$$

Interpretation:

- $\mathcal{PP}_s(E)>0$ → recursion tends inward in potential‑space (potential attractor‑seeking)  
- $\mathcal{PP}_s(E)<0$ → recursion tends outward in potential‑space (potential repeller‑seeking)  
- $\mathcal{PP}_s(E)=0$ → potential‑neutral epoch  

The recursion potential captures **directional tendency of recursion in potential geometry**.

Define the potential recursion basin of epoch $E$:

$$
\mathcal{B}_{\mathcal{PP}}(E)
