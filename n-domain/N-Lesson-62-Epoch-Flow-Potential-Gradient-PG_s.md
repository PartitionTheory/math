# N‑Lesson‑62 — Epoch Flow Potential Gradient $\mathcal{PG}_s$

## 1. From Potential Recursion to Potential Gradient

In N‑Lesson‑61 we introduced the potential recursion potential:

$$
\mathcal{PP}_s=-\mathcal{CP}_s
$$

which measures directional tendency of recursion in potential‑space.

Now we introduce the **epoch flow potential gradient**:

$$\mathcal{PG}_s$$

The potential gradient measures **how the recursion potential changes across the potential manifold** $\mathcal{M}_s$.

Where $\mathcal{PP}_s$ describes directional tendency, $\mathcal{PG}_s$ describes **spatial variation**.

---

## 2. Defining the Epoch Flow Potential Gradient

Let $\mathcal{P}_s$ be the recursion potential field.

The potential gradient is defined as:

$$
\mathcal{PG}_s(E)=\nabla(\mathcal{PP}_s(E))
$$

Interpretation:

- $\mathcal{PG}_s(E)>0$ → potential recursion tendency increases in that direction  
- $\mathcal{PG}_s(E)<0$ → potential recursion tendency decreases in that direction  
- $\mathcal{PG}_s(E)=0$ → gradient‑neutral epoch  

The gradient captures **spatial variation of recursion tendency in potential‑space**.

---

## 3. Spatial Variation of Recursion Potential

The potential gradient reveals:

- how attractor influence strengthens or weakens across potential geometry  
- how repeller influence expands or contracts  
- how recursion tendency accelerates or decelerates in potential‑space  
- how structural features create potential slopes  
- how recursion transitions between high‑potential and low‑potential regions  

Epochs with high $|\mathcal{PG}_s|$ exhibit strong spatial variation in potential recursion tendency.

---

## 4. Potential Gradient Basins

Define the potential gradient basin of epoch $E$:

$$
\mathcal{B}_{\mathcal{PG}}(E)
    =\{E'\in\mathcal{E}\mid 
        \text{sign}(\mathcal{PG}_s(E'))=\text{sign}(\mathcal{PG}_s(E))
     \}
$$

This partitions the epoch domain into:

- positive gradient basins  
- negative gradient basins  
- gradient‑neutral basins  

These refine the recursion basins introduced in N‑Lesson‑61.

$$\Delta\mathcal{PG}$$

### Global potential gradient density

$$
\Delta\mathcal{PG}_{global}
    =\frac{measure(\mathcal{PG}_s)}{measure(\mathcal{M}_s)}
$$

### Local potential gradient density

For any region $R\subseteq N$:

$$
\Delta\mathcal{PG}(R)
    =\frac{measure(\mathcal{PG}_s\cap\mathcal{M}_s(R))}
           {measure(\mathcal{M}_s(R))}
$$

This reveals where spatial variation of recursion tendency in potential‑space concentrates across the epoch manifold.
