# N‑Lesson‑56 — Epoch Flow Potential Anisotropy Tensor $\mathcal{AP}_s$

## 1. From Potential Shear to Potential Anisotropy

In N‑Lesson‑55 we introduced the potential shear operator:

$$
\mathcal{ShP}_s=\frac{1}{2}\left(\mathcal{JP}_s+\mathcal{JP}_s^{T}\right)
$$

which measures directional distortion of recursion potential.

Now we introduce the **epoch flow potential anisotropy tensor**:

$$\mathcal{AP}_s$$

The potential anisotropy tensor measures **directional imbalance** in recursion potential across the manifold $\mathcal{M}_s$.

Where shear describes distortion, anisotropy describes **unequal directional behavior** of the potential field.

---

## 2. Defining the Epoch Flow Potential Anisotropy Tensor

Let $V_s$ be the epoch flow vector field.

The potential anisotropy tensor is defined as:

$$
\mathcal{AP}_s(E)=\mathcal{JP}_s(E)-\mathcal{ShP}_s(E)
$$

Interpretation:

- $\mathcal{AP}_s(E)>0$ → directional imbalance increases  
- $\mathcal{AP}_s(E)<0$ → directional imbalance decreases  
- $\mathcal{AP}_s(E)=0$ → anisotropy‑neutral epoch  

The anisotropy tensor captures **non‑uniform directional deformation** of recursion potential.

---

## 3. Directional Imbalance of Recursion Potential

The potential anisotropy tensor reveals:

- how potential favors certain directions  
- how attractor influence becomes directionally biased  
- how repeller influence becomes uneven  
- how potential geometry becomes directionally asymmetric  
- how recursion transitions between isotropic and anisotropic potential regimes  

Epochs with high $|\mathcal{AP}_s|$ exhibit strong directional imbalance.

---

## 4. Anisotropy Basins

Define the potential anisotropy basin of epoch $E$:

$$
\mathcal{B}_{\mathcal{AP}}(E)
    =\{E'\in\mathcal{E}\mid 
        sign(\mathcal{AP}_s(E'))=sign(\mathcal{AP}_s(E))
     \}
$$

This partitions the epoch domain into:

- positive anisotropy basins  
- negative anisotropy basins  
- anisotropy‑neutral basins  

These refine the shear basins introduced in N‑Lesson‑55.

$$\Delta\mathcal{AP}$$

### Global potential anisotropy density

$$
\Delta\mathcal{AP}_{global}

For any region $R\subseteq N$:

$$
\Delta\mathcal{AP}(R)
    =\frac{measure(\mathcal{AP}_s\cap\mathcal{M}_s(R))}
           {measure(\mathcal{M}_s(R))}
$$

This reveals where directional imbalance of recursion potential concentrates across the epoch manifold.
