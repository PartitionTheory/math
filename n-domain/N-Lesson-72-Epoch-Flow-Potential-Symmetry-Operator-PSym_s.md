## 2. Defining the Epoch Flow Potential Symmetry Operator

Let $\mathcal{PAn}_s$ be the potential anisotropy tensor.

Define the symmetry operator as the **invariance projector** on anisotropy:

$$
\mathcal{PSym}_s(E)
=\mathcal{PAn}_s(E)-\mathcal{PAn}_s(E)_{break}
$$

where:

- $\mathcal{PAn}_s(E)_{break}$ is the component of anisotropy that breaks a chosen symmetry group $G_s$  
- $\mathcal{PSym}_s(E)$ is the component **compatible** with $G_s$

Interpretation:

- large $\|\mathcal{PSym}_s(E)\|$ → strong symmetry‑compatible anisotropy  
- $\mathcal{PSym}_s(E)=0$ → fully symmetry‑broken anisotropy  

The symmetry operator captures **how much of the potential’s directional structure respects a chosen symmetry**.
