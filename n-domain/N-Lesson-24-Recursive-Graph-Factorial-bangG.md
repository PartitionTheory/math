## 2. Definition of Recursive Graph Factorial (!G)

Let N be a structural domain, and let p be a partition index.

Definition:

!G(p) = Gₛ(p) ×G Gₛ(Ω(p)) ×G Gₛ(Ω²(p)) ×G … ×G Gₛ(Ω^{ΔG(p)}(p))

Where:
- Gₛ(p) is the adjacency graph generated from p,
- ΩG determines successor graphs,
- ΔG(p) determines recursion depth,
- ×G performs graph‑level multiplication.

In words:
!G is repeated graph‑level multiplication along the ΩG‑chain, with recursion depth determined by 
graph density ΔG.
