# N‑Lesson‑0016 — Structural Flows and Multi-Step Optimization
Ambient Flow Fields and Discrete Structural Evolution

---

## 1. Purpose

Lessons 0011–0015 introduced ambient derivatives (gradient, Jacobian, Hessian),
ambient Laplacians and divergence, and discrete optimisation rules on P.

Lesson 0016 develops **structural flows**:

• ambient flow fields in ℓ²,  
• adjacency-based structural transitions,  
• multi-step optimisation trajectories,  
• curvature-aware and Laplacian-smoothed flow guidance.

All flows on P are discrete; ambient flows provide guidance.

---

## 2. Ambient Flow Field

Let F(p) = Φ(ψ(p)) with Φ differentiable on an open neighbourhood of ψ(P).

Define the ambient flow direction:

    X(p) := −∇F(p) ∈ ℓ².

This is the ambient steepest-descent direction. It is not itself a structural
path in P.

---

## 3. Structural Flow Graph

Let 𝒩(p) ⊆ P be the finite adjacency neighbourhood of p under the unit-step
relation of Lesson 0001.

A structural flow step must move to some q ∈ 𝒩(p).

---

## 4. Structural Displacements

For q ∈ 𝒩(p), define the displacement:

    δ(p, q) = ψ(q) − ψ(p) ∈ ℓ².

This is the only notion of “direction” available on P.

---

## 5. Ambient Projection onto Structural Moves

Define the ambient linear estimate:

    P(p, q) := ⟨∇F(p), δ(p, q)⟩.

Interpretation:

• P(p, q) < 0 → displacement aligned with ambient descent,  
• P(p, q) > 0 → displacement opposed to descent,  
• P(p, q) = 0 → orthogonal.

Minimising P(p, q) selects neighbours most aligned with ambient descent.

This is a **first-order heuristic**: it does not guarantee F(q) ≤ F(p).

---

## 6. Structural Flow Step (Heuristic)

Ambient-guided structural flow:

    p_{n+1} ∈ argmin_{q ∈ 𝒩(p_n)} P(p_n, q).

This selects the neighbour whose displacement has the most favourable ambient
linear estimate. Actual decrease must be checked via F(q).

---

## 7. Monotone Structural Flow

Define the monotone descent set:

    D(p) := { q ∈ 𝒩(p) : F(q) ≤ F(p) }.

Define the monotone flow step:

    p_{n+1} ∈ argmin_{q ∈ D(p_n)} P(p_n, q).

This ensures:

• structural validity,  
• monotonicity,  
• ambient alignment among monotone neighbours.

---

## 8. Multi-Step Structural Flow Trajectories

A structural flow trajectory is a sequence:

    p_0 → p_1 → p_2 → ⋯

generated by repeated application of a flow rule.

Trajectories:

• remain in P,  
• follow adjacency edges,  
• are guided by ambient flow,  
• may converge, plateau, or cycle depending on neighbourhood geometry.

---

## 9. Second-Order Flow Guidance

If Φ is twice Fréchet differentiable, define:

    Q(p, q) := HF(p)[δ(p, q), δ(p, q)]
             = ⟨T_H(p) δ(p, q), δ(p, q)⟩.

Interpretation:

• Q > 0 → convex along δ,  
• Q < 0 → concave along δ,  
• Q = 0 → flat along δ.

A curvature-aware flow prefers:

• P(p, q) < 0,  
• Q(p, q) small or negative,  
• F(q) ≤ F(p).

---

## 10. Laplacian-Smoothed Flow (Corrected)

The discrete structural Laplacian from Lesson 0014 is:

    Δ_struct F(p) = Σ_{r ∈ 𝒩(p)} (F(r) − F(p)).

To make smoothing neighbour-dependent, define:

    S(p, q) := Δ_struct F(q).

A Laplacian-smoothed flow step is:

    p_{n+1} ∈ argmin_{q ∈ 𝒩(p_n)} ( P(p_n, q), S(p_n, q) ).

This incorporates neighbourhood variation around each candidate q.

---

## 11. Examples

### Example 1 — Quadratic Magnitude

Φ(x) = ‖x‖₂², F(p) = ‖ψ(p)‖₂².

    ∇F(p) = 2ψ(p),
    X(p) = −2ψ(p),
    P(p, q) = 2⟨ψ(p), δ(p, q)⟩,
    Q(p, q) = 2‖δ(p, q)‖₂².

Flow moves toward neighbours whose embeddings lie closer to the origin.

---

### Example 2 — Weighted Quadratic

Φ(x) = Σ w_{ij} x_{ij}² with w ∈ ℓ∞.

    ∇F(p)_{ij} = 2 w_{ij} ψ(p)_{ij},
    T_H(p) u = (2 w_{ij} u_{ij})_{i,j}.

Flow prefers neighbours reducing weighted coordinates.

---

### Example 3 — Bounded Linear Functional

Φ : ℓ² → ℝ bounded linear.  
By Riesz, Φ(x) = ⟨g, x⟩.

    ∇F(p) = g,
    X(p) = −g,
    P(p, q) = ⟨g, δ(p, q)⟩,
    Q(p, q) = 0.

Flow is purely first-order.
