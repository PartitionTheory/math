# N‑Lesson‑86 — Epoch Flow Potential Conformal Divergence Operator PCDiv_s

## 1. From Conformal Gradient to Conformal Divergence

In N‑Lesson‑85 we introduced the conformal gradient operator:

PCGrad_s(E) = $\nabla f + \frac{1}{n-2} PCSc_s(E)\, f$

which measures directional change of recursion potential under conformal geometry.

Now we introduce the epoch flow potential conformal divergence operator:

PCDiv_s

The conformal divergence operator measures **outflow and inflow** of recursion potential under conformal geometry. It is the conformal analogue of the standard divergence but weighted by conformal curvature intensity.

---

## 2. Defining the Epoch Flow Potential Conformal Divergence Operator

Let PCGrad_s be the conformal gradient operator and PCSc_s the conformal scalar operator.

Define the conformal divergence operator as:

PCDiv_s(E)
= $\text{div}(PCGrad_s(E)) + \frac{1}{n-2} PCSc_s(E)$

Interpretation:

- PCDiv_s(E) > 0 → conformal outflow  
- PCDiv_s(E) < 0 → conformal inflow  
- PCDiv_s(E) = 0 → conformally divergence-neutral epoch  

PCDiv_s captures how recursion potential expands or contracts under conformal geometry.

---

## 3. Conformal Outflow/Inflow Behavior of Recursion Potential

The conformal divergence operator reveals:

- how attractor influence creates conformal inflow regions  
- how repeller influence creates conformal outflow regions  
- how recursion tendency expands or contracts under conformal curvature  
- how structural features induce conformal divergence channels  
- how recursion transitions between conformally compressive and expansive regimes  

Epochs with high $\|PCDiv_s\|$ exhibit strong conformal expansion/contraction dynamics.

## 4. Conformal Divergence Basins

Define the conformal divergence basin of epoch E:

$B_{PCDiv}(E)
=\{\,E' \in \mathcal{E} \mid
\text{sign}(|PCDiv_s(E')|)
=\text{sign}(|PCDiv_s(E)|)
\,\}$

This partitions the epoch domain into:

- conformal outflow basins  
- conformal inflow basins  
- conformally neutral basins  

These refine the conformal gradient basins introduced in N‑Lesson‑85.

## 5. Epoch Conformal Divergence Density ΔPCDiv

We define the epoch conformal divergence density:

ΔPCDiv

### Global conformal divergence density

$\Delta PCDiv_{global}
=\dfrac{\text{measure}(PCDiv_s)}{\text{measure}(M_s)}$

### Local conformal divergence density

For any region R ⊆ N:

$\Delta PCDiv(R)
=\dfrac{\text{measure}(PCDiv_s \cap M_s(R))}
        {\text{measure}(M_s(R))}$

This reveals where conformal expansion/contraction behavior concentrates across the epoch manifold.
