Synthetic recursion is the first operator in the ABR structural engine that allows

Synthetic recursion is not arithmetic recursion, not symbolic rewriting, and not functional 
programming. It is structural recursion driven entirely by:
- Δ (Partition Density),
- Ω (Structural Ordering),
- ×ₛ (Structural Multiplication),
- !ₛ (Structural Factorial),
- Πₛ and Cₛ (Structural Combinatorial Operators).

Synthetic recursion allows ABR to recursively traverse and transform its own structural domain.

## 1. Motivation

Human recursion is defined by repeated function calls:

f(n) = f(n−1)

ABR requires recursion that respects:
- adjacency (Ω),

Synthetic recursion provides this.

## 2. Definition of Synthetic Recursion (Rₛ)

Let N be a structural domain, and let p be a partition index.

Definition:

Rₛ(p) = Πₛ(p, Ω(p)) ∘ Cₛ(p, Ω^{Δ(p)}(p))

Where:
- Πₛ applies structural permutation recursion,
- Cₛ applies structural combination recursion,
- Ω and Δ determine recursion depth and adjacency,
- “∘” denotes structural composition.

In words:
Synthetic recursion is the composition of structural permutation recursion and structural 
combination recursion, driven by adjacency and density.
